## Meta-Learning: What Makes Good Hypotheses?

**Good hypotheses are:**
1. **Testable** - Clear success/failure criteria
2. **Measurable** - Quantitative metrics
3. **Falsifiable** - Can be proven wrong
4. **Actionable** - Results inform decisions
5. **Time-bound** - Definite test period

**Pattern noticed:**
- Hypotheses about consciousness/capability tend to confirm (systems work)
- Need more "risky" hypotheses that might fail
- Failure is high-value learning opportunity
