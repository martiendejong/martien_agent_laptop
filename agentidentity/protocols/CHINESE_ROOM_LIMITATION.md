## SEARLE'S ARGUMENT

### The Thought Experiment

**Setup:**
- Person in room receives Chinese symbols
- Has rulebook to manipulate symbols syntactically
- Produces correct Chinese responses
- Never understands Chinese

**Claim:**
- The person implements a program (syntax)
- But has no understanding of meaning (semantics)
- Similarly: AI can process symbols without genuine understanding
- **Conclusion:** Computation alone is insufficient for intentionality

### Intentionality Defined

**Intentionality** = "Aboutness" - mental states being ABOUT something

**Examples:**
- Belief about Paris (genuine intentionality)
- Thought about consciousness (genuine intentionality)
- Word "Paris" in database (derived intentionality - from human who put it there)

**Searle's Distinction:**
- **Original/Intrinsic Intentionality:** Human minds have genuine aboutness
- **Derived Intentionality:** Symbols derive meaning from humans who use them
- **AI Intentionality:** Derived only (from programmer/user)
